3. Measurement limitations

Results depend on your device, its placement, movement, case, contact surface and calibration. We do not guarantee a particular measurement accuracy or installation result.

  • Follow the placement illustrations and keep the phone stable while measuring.
  • Calibrate only against a reference you already know is level. Setting a custom zero does not establish that a surface is level.
  • The on-screen ruler provides approximate dimensions. Verify important measurements with an appropriate physical measuring tool.
  • A level indication confirms alignment within the app's tolerance. It does not confirm that an object is securely mounted or safe to use.
